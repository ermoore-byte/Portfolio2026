Challenge

Approach

Approach

A Self-Service Training Catalog for Partner Bank Sales Teams

CLIENT: Fiserv · FI Sales Training Manager
Challenge

Partner bank sales teams across KeyBank, TD, Zions affiliates, and Hancock Whitney had no self-service reference, driving a steady stream of repeat training requests.

Approach

Repurposed recorded commercialization calls into a bite-sized, self-paced product training catalog available 24/7 ahead of merchant conversations.

Result

Monthly training request volume dropped by half as teams gained on-demand access to product answers, earning multiple internal recognitions.
